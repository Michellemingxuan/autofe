# Others

The four engines are scaffolds, not a fence. This one is for the idea that
none of them would produce: a different source of knowledge, a different way
of reasoning, a hunch with a reason behind it. Use it to be creative - and
say plainly what the route was.

## How to use it

1. **Name the route.** Before the idea, say where it comes from and why the
   four engines would miss it.
2. **Make it concrete**: a measure on data in reach, at the model's grain.
3. **Keep it honest**: a route you cannot name is not a route. "It might
   work" is not one; "collections staff flag this first" is.

## Routes worth trying

* **Practitioner knowledge** (an example of this engine, not its limit) -
  the people who work these accounts recognise trouble before any model does.
  Take a seat and ask what they notice first:
  * a collections officer - who stops answering, who switches how they pay;
  * an underwriter - what a file looks like before it goes bad;
  * a fraud analyst - what a bust-out looks like on the way up;
  * the customer - what they change first when money gets tight.
  Write the heuristic as they would say it, then as a measure.
* **Earlier model generations** - the model history (in the brief, when the
  analyst keeps one) lists variables earlier generations used or dropped, and
  why. A dropped variable is proposed again only with a change that answers
  why it was dropped.
* **The product and its rules** - statement cycles, minimum-payment rules,
  limit changes, fees: behaviour bends around them, and the bends are signal.
* **The market** - a rate rise, a sector in trouble, a regional shock: who in
  the book is exposed to it.
* **Anything else** - as long as the route has a name.

## Example idea

**Collections heuristic: "customers who suddenly switch payment channel"**
(practitioner knowledge) - the channel of the latest payments differs from the
customer's usual one, for example autopay to manual, or online to branch.

## In the idea

`engine: others`. In the description, name the route first - "practitioner
(collections): ...", "dropped in Gen7 for instability, now with ...".
